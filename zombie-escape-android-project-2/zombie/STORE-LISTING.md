## English (default)
**Title:** Snow Zombie Escape
**Short description:** Draw a path through the snow and save her from the zombie!
**Full description:**
A zombie is chasing a girl across the snow, and only you can save her!

Draw with your finger to clear a path through the terrain. She runs wherever you clear, so think fast, dodge the zombie, collect loot, and reach the flag 🚩 or hide in a house 🏠.

• 200 levels, with a new world every 5 levels: snow, water, trees, rocks, gold, sheep, mud, glass and volcano
• 12 characters to unlock
• Simple one-finger controls
• 10 languages
• Works offline

Can you get her to safety?
